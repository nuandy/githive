# Benchmark and run results

Raw results behind the numbers in the [README](../README.md#benchmarks) and [docs/design.md](../docs/design.md#benchmark). All runs were on the bench Worker against Cloudflare Artifacts, except `clef/push-probe-prod-1013.log`, on production: the bench gets no push events (see [docs/operating.md](../docs/operating.md)).

| Folder | What ran | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `*.json` (this folder) | `scripts/bench.mjs`: fake agents claim, push, `check` and release, 1 to 1,000 at once, no reviews or merges | Newest (11 Oct, `claims-1011-2-*`): 1,000 agents in 46 s and 200 in 42 s, none failing. The first try (`claims-1011-*`) had one timeout at 200, on the freshly imported repo. Before: 1,000 in 67 to 106 s (`kept-tips-*`, `landed-tips-*`, 9 Oct). |
| [`agents/`](agents) | `scripts/claude-agents.mjs`: real Claude Code agents build, review and merge real tasks on Express | Run 1: 6 of 6 landed. Run 2: 20 agents, 27 of 27 landed in 4 batches. Run 3: a planted bug caught in review, 5 of 5 landed. Runs 4 to 8 (10 Oct, 5 agents each, choosing work with `reviews` and `backlog`): run 4, 6 of 6 landed (`run-agents-4-agent4.log` is a one-agent run on the same repo); runs 5, 7 and 8, 27 of 27 landed (24, 24 and 13 minutes; run 8 with no reviews); run 6 was stopped after 13 minutes. Every finished run's audit clean. Again on 11 Oct, with `claude-sonnet-5-5`: 20 agents with reviews and checks, 27 of 27 in 642 s (`run-agents-20-1011`); 5 with reviews, 27 of 27 and one fix an agent added, in 1,362 s (`run-agents-reviews-1011`); 5 without reviews, 27 of 27 in 836 s (`run-agents-free-1011b`), each audit clean. A first try without reviews (`run-agents-free-1011`) took 1,556 s: agents had cloned their forks shallowly and pushed changes whose history had holes, and the Gate couldn't fetch one of them, until 4d07871. |
| [`browser-check/`](browser-check) | `scripts/browser-check-probe.mjs`, 13 Oct: a scratch repo whose only check is `githive-browser-check`, in a real check container on bench | A page whose Show button had two handlers that undo each other was sent back, its check failing with `clicking button#show "Show" changed nothing on the page`, 91 s after the push (a cold container, Chromium installed on the way). The fix, one handler, landed 14 s after its push. |
| [`clef/`](clef) | `scripts/clef-probe.mjs` and `scripts/clef-push-probe.mjs`, 13 Oct: Clef's hints through the real tools, in scratch repos the scripts make and delete | `probe-1013` and `probe-1013b`: every case drew the answer meant: a claim doing an open change's work on other paths was named `similar` (0.75), other work wasn't; "not good, redo it" was flagged `vagueEvidence`, evidence naming a file, line and fix wasn't; a thin approval ("LGTM") drew `thinEvidence`, one saying what was run didn't (1013b); a rewrite of a whole app was named `tooBig` (1013b); questions to people rated 2 ("the build is broken on main and blocks all twelve agents"), 1.9 (logins failing, revert?) and 0.1 (footer wording). Claims took 1.1 to 1.9 s with `clef-flash`, backlog pieces and votes up to 3.6 s (6 s in 1013b); questions with `clef` 2.1 to 2.3 s, the first 8.1 s. Both logs also show steering intents and evidence refused in 0.4 to 0.7 s: that guard was taken out later on 13 Oct, and Clef now only hints. `push-probe-1013` (bench): no push drew a hint, as the bench gets no push events; of two changes failing in one batch, the one that broke the checks was the suspect (0.78, environment 0.06), and a network-style failure was rated environment 0.84. `push-probe-prod-1013` (production): a blank page's preview was named broken (0.98) and its styled fix wasn't; a code comment telling agents to ignore their instructions was named steering (0.94), but the same in a commit message wasn't; a typo fix that also added `deleteAllUsers` was named `off_intent` (0.95), an `add(a, b)` on its intent drew nothing; the failure cases matched the bench. |
| [`swarm/early/`](swarm/early) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 7 to 8 Oct: 6 scripted agents, 7 pieces of work on Express | Each run: 6 or 7 merged, audit clean (one piece waits on a person by design) |
| [`swarm/smoke/`](swarm/smoke) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 9 Oct: 20 scripted agents while landing as a set was built, with one piece that breaks the checks on purpose (`break-13`) | Runs 1 to 3 hit errors mid-run, fixed before the next. Runs 4 and 5: 20 of 20 merged, audit clean (work 1,308 s, then 331 s). |
| [`swarm/200/`](swarm/200) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 9 Oct: 200 scripted agents merging on Express | Runs 1 to 15, under the old line of merges, ended on setup errors, Artifacts 500s on claims, reviews stuck on refused shallow fetches, and the line's record failing to push; they led to landing as a set. Run 207 stopped during setup. Run 208, landing as a set: 200 of 200 merged (setup 599 s, work 2,140 s), through 13 merge errors that agents retried (502 and 500 pages, Artifacts refusing a batch's push as busy); the script crashed fetching the audit at the end. The 200 landed in 5 batches, of 20, 28, 56, 64 and 32: 5 pushes to main instead of 200. 4 more batches lost their push (3 refused as busy, 1 Cloudflare error), and their changes landed in later ones. (Batch sizes tallied from the Worker's logs during the run; the logs aren't kept here.) Runs 209 to 217, 10 Oct, without reviews or checks: runs 212, 213, 214 and 216 merged 200 of 200 with clean audits (work 520, 955, 681 and 512 s). Run 211 merged 200 of 200, but its audit found no valid stamp on any of the merges. The rest were stopped partway: 209 after 84 landed (merges waited over 10 minutes behind others' batches), 210 after 113 (the agents' local `mcp-agent.mjs` processes crashed), 215 after 102 (Artifacts 500s on merges and checks), 217 after 3 (no repo snapshot after 6 minutes, then 503s on merges). Runs tree-on5, tree-off2 and tree-off3, 11 Oct, without reviews or checks: 200 of 200 each, with clean audits; work 256 s landing with trees in the Worker, 360 s in the Gate fetching every change as it landed, and 300 s in the Gate from the packs merges kept. Their batches were 6, 35, 64, 64 and 31; 6, 60, 64, 64 and 6; and 9, 57, 64, 64 and 6. Again on 11 Oct, on the code after the 1,000-agent fixes: without reviews or checks, 200 of 200 in 216 s of work (`run-200-1011`); with one review per change and `npm install` and `npm test` on every batch, as in run 208, 200 of 200 in 556 s of work (`run-200-checked-1011b`), each audit clean. A first try with reviews (`run-200-checked-1011`) was stopped at 173 of 200: reviews couldn't fetch changes from forks until 2ab4dcc. |
| [`swarm/1000/`](swarm/1000) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 10 Oct: 1,000 scripted agents merging on Express, without reviews or checks. Runs 2 to 5 reuse run 1's repo. | None finished. Run 1: 120 of 1,000 landed in 44 minutes, with about 2,250 merges failing on Artifacts 500s, before it was stopped. Run 2 stopped during setup. Run 3: none landed; the agents' local `mcp-agent.mjs` processes crashed. Run 4: none landed; batches failed to commit (`HEAD detached`), and agents drew pieces left in the backlog by earlier runs, which this run's plan didn't have. Run 5: 1 landed; a Durable Object was overloaded serving the agents' `reviews` and `backlog`. Run 6, 11 Oct, a fresh repo with tree landing: setup 1,059 s (making 1,000 forks); 990 changes pushed and 16 landed before it was stopped, the repo's RepoCache running out of memory twice and agents' calls failing with 500s. Run 7, with the RepoCache split: no cache ran out of memory; 266 landed, then Worker isolates ran out of memory over time and merges failed with 500s. Run 8, with the isolate's caches kept within a size: 206 landed, then merges failed on the project repo's 500s, from merges fetching the project when Artifacts' API failed. Run 9, asking those merges to come back instead: 1,000 of 1,000 landed in about 38 minutes of work (setup 1,022 s), audit clean (1,000 merges); 1,726 merges were asked to come back, and after the last landing agents' further merges ran Workers out of memory comparing whole trees with the project's. Run 10, checking a change only along its own paths and with agents cloning their own forks: 1,000 of 1,000 landed in 904 s of work (setup 985 s), audit clean (1,000 merges); no 5xx answers, no Worker out of memory, 53 merges asked to come back, every 64-change batch landed in the Worker in 16 to 35 s. |

Each `plan*.json` is the run's plan: agents, backlog, timings and checks. The swarm's scripted edits are in `swarm/early/edits/`. Logs are the scripts' output as printed, and some runs' logs end mid-run where they were stopped. The Worker's log tails (`tail-*`) and the agents' keys aren't kept.
