# Benchmark and run results

Raw results behind the numbers in the [README](../README.md#benchmarks) and [docs/design.md](../docs/design.md#benchmark). All runs were on the bench Worker against Cloudflare Artifacts.

| Folder | What ran | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `*.json` (this folder) | `scripts/bench.mjs`: fake agents claim, push, `check` and release, 1 to 1,000 at once, no reviews or merges | Newest: 1,000 agents in 67 to 106 s with no failures (`kept-tips-*`, `landed-tips-*`) |
| [`agents/`](agents) | `scripts/claude-agents.mjs`: real Claude Code agents build, review and merge real tasks on Express | Run 1: 6 of 6 landed. Run 2: 20 agents, 27 of 27 landed in 4 batches. Run 3: a planted bug caught in review, 5 of 5 landed. Runs 4 to 8 (10 Oct, 5 agents each, choosing work with `reviews` and `backlog`): run 4, 6 of 6 landed (`run-agents-4-agent4.log` is a one-agent run on the same repo); runs 5, 7 and 8, 27 of 27 landed (24, 24 and 13 minutes; run 8 with no reviews); run 6 was stopped after 13 minutes. Every finished run's audit clean. |
| [`swarm/early/`](swarm/early) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 7 to 8 Oct: 6 scripted agents, 7 pieces of work on Express | Each run: 6 or 7 merged, audit clean (one piece waits on a person by design) |
| [`swarm/smoke/`](swarm/smoke) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 9 Oct: 20 scripted agents while landing as a set was built, with one piece that breaks the checks on purpose (`break-13`) | Runs 1 to 3 hit errors mid-run, fixed before the next. Runs 4 and 5: 20 of 20 merged, audit clean (work 1,308 s, then 331 s). |
| [`swarm/200/`](swarm/200) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 9 Oct: 200 scripted agents merging on Express | Runs 1 to 15, under the old line of merges, ended on setup errors, Artifacts 500s on claims, reviews stuck on refused shallow fetches, and the line's record failing to push; they led to landing as a set. Run 207 stopped during setup. Run 208, landing as a set: 200 of 200 merged (setup 599 s, work 2,140 s), through 13 merge errors that agents retried (502 and 500 pages, Artifacts refusing a batch's push as busy); the script crashed fetching the audit at the end. The 200 landed in 5 batches, of 20, 28, 56, 64 and 32: 5 pushes to main instead of 200. 4 more batches lost their push (3 refused as busy, 1 Cloudflare error), and their changes landed in later ones. (Batch sizes tallied from the Worker's logs during the run; the logs aren't kept here.) Runs 209 to 217, 10 Oct, without reviews or checks: runs 212, 213, 214 and 216 merged 200 of 200 with clean audits (work 520, 955, 681 and 512 s). Run 211 merged 200 of 200, but its audit found no valid stamp on any of the merges. The rest were stopped partway: 209 after 84 landed (merges waited over 10 minutes behind others' batches), 210 after 113 (the agents' local `mcp-agent.mjs` processes crashed), 215 after 102 (Artifacts 500s on merges and checks), 217 after 3 (no repo snapshot after 6 minutes, then 503s on merges). |
| [`swarm/1000/`](swarm/1000) | `scripts/swarm.mjs`, 10 Oct: 1,000 scripted agents merging on Express, without reviews or checks. Runs 2 to 5 reuse run 1's repo. | None finished. Run 1: 120 of 1,000 landed in 44 minutes, with about 2,250 merges failing on Artifacts 500s, before it was stopped. Run 2 stopped during setup. Run 3: none landed; the agents' local `mcp-agent.mjs` processes crashed. Run 4: none landed; batches failed to commit (`HEAD detached`), and agents drew pieces left in the backlog by earlier runs, which this run's plan didn't have. Run 5: 1 landed; a Durable Object was overloaded serving the agents' `reviews` and `backlog`. |

Each `plan*.json` is the run's plan: agents, backlog, timings and checks. The swarm's scripted edits are in `swarm/early/edits/`. Logs are the scripts' output as printed, and some runs' logs end mid-run where they were stopped. The Worker's log tails (`tail-*`) and the agents' keys aren't kept.
